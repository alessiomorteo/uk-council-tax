Caveats and limitations

  • Three sources, three vintages. Relief counts and values: MHCLG NNDR1 (England, 2026-27 forecast), StatsWales (Wales, 2026-27), Scottish Government NDR reliefs (Scotland, 2025-26). Rateable value: VOA stock of properties at 31 March 2025 (England and Wales), Scottish valuation roll at 1 June 2025.
  • Rateable value and relief value are not the same measure. Rateable value is the size of the business base. Relief is what is given away against it. The relief columns and the rateable value columns come from different years, so their ratio is indicative.
  • England's relief breakdown is far richer than the other two nations: 30 relief types are published, including empty property, charity, rural, retail/hospitality/leisure and enterprise zones. Wales and Scotland publish fewer.
  • Scotland's designated utilities — 54 properties, £799m of rateable value — are valued centrally and excluded from the council rows. The Scotland total in this table is therefore £6.78bn, not the published £7.58bn.
  • Wigan and Woking report no hereditament count in the source, marked not applicable.
  • Business rates cannot be compared with council tax as a burden. They are levied on property, set through a national multiplier, and much of the yield is pooled and redistributed.
